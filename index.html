Friends of Woodmont Park

Neighbors caring for the shared spaces of Woodmont — starting with the park and courts at the heart of our community.

Who We Are

Friends of Woodmont Park is a newly formed Virginia nonprofit corporation created by and for the residents of the Woodmont neighborhood and surrounding area of North Chesterfield, Virginia.

Our mission is to preserve, protect, and promote the quality of life for all residents through sustainable improvements and collective community effort.

We are in the process of applying for recognition as a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt charitable organization.

Our First Project: The Old Tennis Courts

Behind the Woodmont Recreation Association pool sits a pair of tennis courts that nature has slowly reclaimed. We see something better: a safe, welcoming, multi-use community space.

Near term

Cleanup days, weed and brush removal, drainage fixes, and boardwalk repairs to make the grounds safe and usable again.

Long term

A modular multi-use sport court — pickleball, basketball, and flexible event space — shaped by what neighbors actually want.

How You Can Help

We're just getting started, and we'll need our community behind us. Here's what we'll be looking for:

  • Volunteers. Cleanup days, trail work, and event support — many hands make light work.
  • Grant writers & fundraisers. Know your way around a grant application? We have funding opportunities (community and corporate grants) ready to pursue once our tax-exempt status lands.
  • Donations. Once our 501(c)(3) status is approved, gifts will be tax-deductible. In the meantime, in-kind help — tools, materials, equipment, and expertise — goes a long way.
  • Your voice. What should this space become? Pickleball? Basketball? A gathering place? Community input will drive the final design.

Why It Matters

Shared spaces are where a neighborhood becomes a community. Restoring the park and courts means a safer place for kids to play, a gathering spot for events and celebrations, and a point of pride that benefits every household in Woodmont — whether or not you ever pick up a pickleball paddle.
