::page-section{id="portal" title="Board portal"}
This is where our board and closest partners keep the work organized: what needs doing, who's on it, what's due, and the discussion that goes with it. We use it during our weekly meetings so everyone sees the same list.

It's open to our directors and to invited partners from the Woodmont Recreation Association. Sign in with the Google account you were invited with.

[Sign in to the board portal](https://admin.woodmontfriends.org){.btn .btn-blaze}

Can't sign in? Email [info@woodmontfriends.org](mailto:info@woodmontfriends.org) and we'll get you set up.
::

::page-section{id="neighbors" title="Not on the board? Follow along." tone="fern"}
Everyone is welcome to see what we're working on. Our [community roadmap](https://github.com/orgs/WoodmontFriends/projects/2) shows what's happening now, what's next, and what's done, and you can [sign up for updates](/#contact) to hear about volunteer days and events.
::
