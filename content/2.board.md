::page-section{id="portal" title="Board portal" tone="fern"}
Our board uses this site to track tasks, who's doing them, and when they're due. We go through it at our weekly meetings.

It's open to our directors and to invited partners from the Woodmont Recreation Association. Sign in with the Google account you were invited with.

[Sign in to the board portal](https://admin.woodmontfriends.org){.btn .btn-blaze}

Can't sign in? Email [info@woodmontfriends.org](mailto:info@woodmontfriends.org) and we'll get you set up.
