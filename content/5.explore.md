::page-section{id="flythrough" title="Take a walk down the trail"}
Follow the trail loop from the footbridge by the pool, down the ravine to the Llama Trotter loop near the train tracks, and back up past the field and the old tennis courts. The whole loop is about a third of a mile.

::page-section{id="explore" title="Explore it yourself" tone="fern"}
Fly over the woods, walk the trail at eye level, or jump to the bridge, the courts, or the pool. You can turn the trees off to see the layout.

::page-section{id="about-the-model" title="About this model"}
We built this model from public data. The ground and the trees come from USGS lidar surveys, so the ravine and the tree heights are real. Buildings and roads come from OpenStreetMap, and neighbors filled in the trails, the bridge, and the courts.

It's a sketch of the park today, not a survey, so positions are approximate. It doesn't show any plans for the old tennis courts. Those will depend on what neighbors want, so [sign up for updates](/#contact) to share your ideas.

Ground and trees from USGS 3DEP lidar. Buildings and roads © [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) contributors.
::
