::page-section{id="donate" title="Support the park"}
Your gift goes straight to work in the park: restoring the old tennis courts, keeping up the trails, and cleaning up along the creek.

We're a volunteer-run 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. You'll get a receipt by email.
