::page-section{id="nature" title="What's living in the park"}
Neighbors have spotted all of this in the park, around the Woodmont neighborhood, and in the woods along the train tracks. Each dot is a photo someone shared on [iNaturalist](https://www.inaturalist.org), where local naturalists help confirm what it is.

Filter by group or area, search for a species, or switch to the heat map to see where things turn up most. Turn on the timeline and press play to watch the seasons change and see whether the deer keep to the same path.

::page-section{id="add-a-sighting" title="Add your own sighting" tone="fern"}
Found something interesting on a walk? Take a photo and share it on iNaturalist. It will show up here within a few minutes.

- **Get the app.** iNaturalist is free for iPhone and Android, or you can upload from a computer at [inaturalist.org](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/upload).
- **Snap a clear photo.** Your phone usually records where you took it. If not, drop a pin on the map when you upload.
- **Make a guess.** The app suggests what it might be, and other naturalists help confirm it.

**A note on mushrooms:** please don't eat anything based on a photo ID. And if you find a good patch, leave plenty for the next neighbor. That's why edible species only show as a rough area on this map.
::
