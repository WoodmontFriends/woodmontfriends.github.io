::page-section{id="about" title="Who we are"}
Friends of Woodmont Park is a nonprofit run by volunteers from the Woodmont neighborhood in North Chesterfield, Virginia. We work with the Woodmont Recreation Association (WRA), which owns the land.

We want the park to be a safe place for kids to play and a good spot for neighborhood events.

We're a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

::page-section{id="plan" title="The park" tone="fern"}
Behind the pool are wooded trails that run back toward the railroad tracks, the lower parking lot, the old tennis courts, and an open field. These areas haven't been kept up in years. With the WRA's permission, we're cleaning them up and turning them into a park for the neighborhood.

  ::trail
    ::trail-stop{title="This fall"}
    Trail cleanup, clearing brush and vines off the old tennis courts, cleaning up the lower parking lot, and fixing the drainage that washes dirt onto the courts.
    ::

    ::trail-stop{title="Next spring"}
    Clearing the field and fixing up the old courts for pickleball, basketball, and events.
    ::

    ::trail-stop{title="Down the road"}
    Bigger projects, like a playground or a youth soccer field, depending on what neighbors want.
    ::
  ::

Our [projects page](/projects) shows what we're working on now and what's next.
