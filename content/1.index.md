::page-section{id="about" title="Who we are"}
Friends of Woodmont Park is a Virginia nonprofit corporation created by and for the residents of the Woodmont neighborhood and surrounding area of North Chesterfield, Virginia. We're a volunteer board of five neighbors, working in partnership with the Woodmont Recreation Association (WRA), which owns the land.

Shared spaces are where a neighborhood becomes a community. Restoring the park means a safer place for kids to play, a gathering spot for events and celebrations, and a point of pride for every household in Woodmont, whether or not you ever pick up a pickleball paddle.

Friends of Woodmont Park is recognized by the IRS as a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt charitable organization, so donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

::page-section{id="plan" title="The park" tone="fern"}
Beyond the pool fence sits land that has waited years for some attention: wooded trails running back toward the railroad tracks, the lower parking lot, the old tennis courts, and an open field. With the WRA's blessing, we're taking on the job of caring for these spaces and turning them into a park the whole neighborhood can enjoy.

  ::trail
    ::trail-stop{title="This fall"}
    Trail maintenance and cleanup, clearing brush and vines from the old tennis courts, tidying the lower parking lot, and tackling the drainage that washes sediment onto the courts.
    ::

    ::trail-stop{title="Next spring"}
    Clearing the field and restoring the old courts into a multi-use space for pickleball, basketball, and gatherings.
    ::

    ::trail-stop{title="Down the road"}
    Bigger ideas like a playground and a field for youth soccer, shaped by what neighbors want most.
    ::
  ::

Want to follow along? See what we're working on now, what's coming next, and what's done on our [projects page](/projects).
