## Who We Are

Friends of Woodmont Park is a Virginia nonprofit corporation created by and for the residents of the Woodmont neighborhood and surrounding area of North Chesterfield, Virginia. We're a volunteer board of five neighbors, working in partnership with the Woodmont Recreation Association (WRA), which owns the land.

Our mission is to **preserve, protect, and promote the quality of life for all residents through sustainable improvements and collective community effort**.

Our application for recognition as a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt charitable organization is underway.

## The Park

Beyond the pool fence sits land that has waited years for some attention: wooded trails running back toward the railroad tracks, the lower parking lot, the old tennis courts, and an open field. With the WRA's blessing, we're taking on the job of caring for these spaces and turning them into a park the whole neighborhood can enjoy.

## How You Can Help

- **Volunteer.** Cleanup days, trail work, and event support. Many hands make light work.
- **Lend tools or expertise.** Mowers, trimmers, trailers, landscaping, drainage, or trail-building know-how all go a long way.
- **Grant writers & fundraisers.** Know your way around a grant application? We'd love your help once our tax-exempt status lands.
- **Donations.** We're setting up our accounts now. Once our 501(c)(3) status is approved, gifts will be tax-deductible.
- **Your voice.** What should this space become? Community input will drive the plans.

Interested? Email us at [info@woodmontfriends.org](mailto:info@woodmontfriends.org).

## Why It Matters

Shared spaces are where a neighborhood becomes a community. Restoring the park means a safer place for kids to play, a gathering spot for events and celebrations, and a point of pride that benefits every household in Woodmont — whether or not you ever pick up a pickleball paddle.
